Game Club is a free, open-source tabletop club for two classic board games:
Ludo and Snakes & Ladders.

Play the way you want:
• Hot-seat: pass one device around the table.
• Bots: easy, medium and hard opponents.
• Online: create a room, share the four-letter code, and play with friends.
• Voice chat, table chat, reconnect and spectator mode are built in.
• Leaderboards track your results across games.
• Ludo supports 2–4 players; Snakes & Ladders supports 2–10.
• Turn timer, autoplay, undo and hints keep a table moving.
• Sound and haptics can be switched off in Settings.

No accounts, no ads, and no tracking. The game works offline for hot-seat and
bot play. Online play uses the project's own free software game server; the
public server is a convenience and can be self-hosted.

Voice chat uses the microphone only while you hold the mic button. Android asks
for microphone access the first time you use it.
